## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a potential security vulnerability, please report it responsibly so that we can investigate and address it.

**How to report:**

- **Preferred method:** Use [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/security-advisories/guidance-on-reporting-and-writing-about-vulnerabilities/about-coordinated-disclosure-of-security-vulnerabilities) feature, if enabled for this repository.
- **Alternative method:** If private vulnerability reporting is unavailable, contact the repository maintainers privately through their publicly listed contact information.

Please do not disclose vulnerabilities publicly through GitHub Issues or public discussions until they have been investigated and addressed.

### What to Include

When reporting a vulnerability, please provide as much of the following information as possible:

- A description of the vulnerability and its potential impact.
- The affected versions, components, or configurations.
- Steps to reproduce the issue or a proof of concept.
- Any suggested mitigations or fixes, if available.

Please avoid including sensitive information, credentials, or private user data in your report.

### Response and Resolution Process

We aim to:

1. **Acknowledge the report:** Within 3 business days of receiving it.
2. **Investigate the issue:** Assess its validity, severity, scope, and potential impact.
3. **Provide updates:** Communicate progress and request additional information when necessary.
4. **Resolve confirmed vulnerabilities:** Develop and test an appropriate fix, prioritizing issues according to their severity.
5. **Coordinate disclosure:** Work with the reporter on responsible public disclosure once a fix or appropriate mitigation is available.

These timelines are targets rather than guarantees and may vary depending on the complexity and severity of the issue.
